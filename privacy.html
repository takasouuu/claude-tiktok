Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-06

Data we collect

When the operator connects one of their TikTok accounts, the Service receives that account's open ID, basic profile information (display name and avatar), and OAuth access and refresh tokens.

How we use it

This data is used only to confirm the connected account and to post the operator's own photos to that account. Photos are stored only for posting and are deleted by the operator when no longer needed. It is not used for any other purpose.

Sharing

We do not sell or share this data with any third party.

Storage and deletion

Tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer. They are deleted when the account is disconnected, and access can be revoked at any time from TikTok's settings (Security > Manage app permissions).
